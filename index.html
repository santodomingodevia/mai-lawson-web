BioBio

Mai Lawson, DJ y productora, comienza su carrera profesional en 2007, llevando su música por clubes de Argentina, América Latina, Estados Unidos, Canadá, Europa, Asia y África.

Con una trayectoria de 20 años en la escena global, es pionera absoluta: la primera DJ argentina en tocar en Burning Man y en ser tapa de Mixmag.

Su música cruzó fronteras y se convirtió en la primera latinoamericana en presentarse en Sri Lanka, frente a 10 mil personas, logrando una presencia global en los cuatro continentes y con fechas próximas en Australia.

Como productora ha alcanzado múltiples veces el top ten y ha editado en sellos como The Soundgarden, Warung Recordings, Gardens of Babylon, Balance y Mango Alley, entre otros.

Ha tocado en los venues más importantes de su país y es considerada una influencia global, lo que la llevó a compartir cabina con Carl Cox, Hernán Cattáneo, Maceo Plex, ARTBAT, Nick Warren, Miss Monique y Argy, entre otros.

Es docente de la Carrera de DJ, ha dado charlas alrededor del mundo y maneja Industria Argentina, su label y ciclo de fiestas.

Mai Lawson, DJ and producer, started her professional career in 2007, taking her music to clubs across Argentina, Latin America, the USA, Canada, Europe, Asia and Africa.

With a 20-year career on the global scene, she is a true pioneer: the first Argentine DJ to play Burning Man and to land the cover of Mixmag.

Her music has crossed borders — she became the first Latin American artist to perform in Sri Lanka, in front of 10,000 people, building a global presence across four continents, with upcoming dates in Australia.

As a producer she has reached the top ten many times, releasing on labels such as The Soundgarden, Warung Recordings, Gardens of Babylon, Balance and Mango Alley, among others.

She has played the most important venues in her country and is considered a global influence, sharing the booth with Carl Cox, Hernán Cattáneo, Maceo Plex, ARTBAT, Nick Warren, Miss Monique and Argy, among others.

She teaches on the professional DJ program, has given talks around the world, and runs Industria Argentina, her label and party series.
